Setting Category Master

Category Master

Group your inventory by type — Raw Material, Semi-Finished and Finished Goods each keep their own category list. A sub-category can only sit under a parent of the same type. Categories are groups only — individual items are created in Inventory › Items.

A Category is a group, not an item. Each type keeps its own list and a sub-category must sit under a parent of the same type. The items themselves (Onion, Amul Butter 500 g) are created in Raw Material, and recipes in Recipe pick a category from here.
Finished Goods categories are for stock & food-cost reporting only and are not shown on the POS. How a finished item appears on the POS menu is controlled by Menu Section.

Menu Section

The groups cashiers and guests see on the POS menu (e.g. Starters, Main Course, Beverages).

A Menu Section is a group on the POS menu that cashiers and guests see (Starters, Main Course, Beverages). It is separate from inventory: a finished item keeps its Category for stock & costing and is mapped to a Menu Section for selling. Finished Goods pick their section in Recipe, so create sections before you add sellable recipes.
ActionsMenu Section NameShown on POS

Recipe

Semi-Finished and Finished Goods recipes — recipe quantity, UOM, yield and ingredients.

A Recipe turns Raw Materials and other Semi-Finished items into something you stock or sell, and its Category comes from Category Master. Finished Goods are sold on the POS, so they also need a Menu Section, a POS unit & conversion and a price. Cost is worked out from the ingredients, so raw material rates must be current.

POS Table Config

Tables and other order channels cashiers pick from when they start an order.

These are the tables and order channels (Takeaway, Home Delivery, Swiggy / Zomato) a cashier chooses from on the POS. They belong to a POS Location, and the Tax group that applies depends on the channel type. Seats and Area apply to seated tables only; anything switched off here disappears from the POS.
